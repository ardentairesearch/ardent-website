01 Our perspectiveThe next layer of intelligence

Intelligence is here.
Agency comes next.

AI can reason, plan, and decide. Turning those decisions into trusted action is the next challenge.

We build the infrastructure in between: the accounts, permissions, and execution systems that let agents operate in the real world.

01 / Identity

A foundation to act.

Every agent needs an identity and an account it can operate through, across tasks and over time.

02 / Control

Freedom within limits.

Useful autonomy starts with clear permissions. Owners define the boundaries. Agents work within them.

03 / Evidence

Proof, not guesswork.

An action is only useful when its result is known. Every execution should leave a verifiable outcome.
